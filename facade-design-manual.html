Facade Design Manual

When I began working at Walters & Wolf, I was eager to learn everything I could about the glass industry. I started creating a booklet to better understand the engineering principles behind curtain wall design, which led me to seek out even more information about the many aspects of our trade. This manual remains a constant work in progress and serves as a way to document the knowledge that is critical to becoming a successful engineer in the glazing industry. It is intended as a practical resource for anyone beginning their journey in the facade industry.
